# Freeport Markets

> Freeport Markets is a Y Combinator-backed fintech and crypto trading platform offering 24/7 self-custody access to stocks, crypto, commodities, forex, rates, perpetual futures and pre-IPO shares, with leverage and AI analysts that turn social and financial media into a real-time feed of market-moving news and actionable trade ideas. The product is delivered primarily through iOS and Android apps. Freeport Markets does not publish a public developer API, OpenAPI, SDKs, or developer documentation at this time.
